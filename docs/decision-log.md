# Decision log

Record 5–10 genuine decisions with the date and actual time in Mauritius (MUT). Do not invent times. Good entries include theme and target user, dataset choice, interface agreement, surprise-card response, scope cuts, and release format.

| Date/time (MUT) | Decision | Why / evidence | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|

For the surprise card, include its exact wording, the resulting change and the check that proves it.
